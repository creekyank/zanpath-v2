The Earth element, in particular, is associated with stability, nourishment, and balance. Earth Day Masters embody these qualities, serving as a stabilizing force in their communities. The cultural background of Chinese metaphysics also plays a significant role, as the emphasis on harmony with nature and the balance of opposites (Yin-Yang) underscores the importance of stability in achieving a prosperous and peaceful life. The Earth element is further divided into different types, such as the mountain and soil elements, each with its unique characteristics and contributions to the overall stability-building process.
